AI is fundamentally changing software engineering. What remains are your **business models and processes**.
z9nai helps companies step into — and transform for — the AI era: close to the customer, with small teams and minimal overhead.

## 🎯 What we focus on
|||
|---|---|
| **Domain Driven Process Orchestration** | Your domain model drives the process — reusable, documented and simulated by design. |
| **BPMN / DMN** | Transparent, maintainable and testable processes in the global standard that business and IT both speak. |
| **Tailored processes** | Built to fit your domain exactly, instead of bending your business around a standard product. |
| **AI-driven development** | Faster from idea to production: analysis, implementation, tests, documentation and refactoring. |
| **Type-safe DSLs** | A clean abstraction between domain and technology — and solid guardrails for AI. |

## 🧰 Open Source

| Project | Description |
|---|---|
| [**Orchescala**](https://github.com/pme123/orchescala) | Domain Driven Process Orchestration toolbox in Scala 3 — generated docs, process simulation and OpenAPI specs from your domain model. [📖 Docs](https://pme123.github.io/orchescala/) |
| [**Camunda DMN Tester**](https://github.com/camunda-community-hub/camunda-dmn-tester) | Test your (Camunda) DMN tables — part of the Camunda Community Hub. The new Version is integrated in _Orchescala_ |
